REHEARSAL CASES FOR THE "NEW CASE (LIVE)" PAGE
Two practice cases written the way an instructor might hand one over: plain paragraphs, not our templates.
All names, dates and facilities are made up. Practise reading each one, deciding which box each part goes in,
and filling in who sent it, before you press Run.

What we expect (do not show this part to the audience):
  Case A should go to the FAST TRACK (Listing 6.03, chronic dialysis).
  Case B should go to the HUMAN QUEUE (no rule is met, and the only strong words come from a family letter).


==============================================================================================================
CASE A
==============================================================================================================

Rosa Delgado filed for SSDI on October 1, 2026. She was born on May 14, 1969, so she was 57 when she applied.
On her application she says she stopped working on July 10, 2026 because of her kidney disease, and that she
has not earned anything since then. She finished high school. For the last 12 years she worked as a school
cafeteria cook. On her disability report she lists two places that treat her: Riverside Nephrology Associates
(Dr. Anand Rao) and Mercy Dialysis Center. She lists her conditions as "kidney failure, on dialysis" and says
she gets tired easily and has swelling in her legs.

The DDS asked Riverside Nephrology Associates for her records, and they sent a clinic note dated
September 22, 2026, signed by Anand Rao, MD, nephrology. The note says: Ms. Delgado has end-stage renal disease
due to long-standing diabetic nephropathy. Her eGFR was 9 in June 2026. She started in-center hemodialysis on
June 30, 2026 and now receives hemodialysis three times a week at Mercy Dialysis Center. Dialysis is expected
to be permanent; she is on the transplant waiting list but no transplant is scheduled. Current medications are
insulin, sevelamer and epoetin. Plan: continue chronic hemodialysis three times weekly.

How to enter it:
  Forms box: the first paragraph. Claim type SSDI, filing date 2026-10-01.
  Medical records box: the second paragraph. How it arrived: requested by the DDS. Provider: Riverside
  Nephrology Associates. Doctor: Anand Rao, credential MD. Date: 2026-09-22.
  Providers on the SSA-3368: Riverside Nephrology Associates, Mercy Dialysis Center.


==============================================================================================================
CASE B
==============================================================================================================

Martin Hale, 44, applied for SSDI on September 28, 2026. He says he has been unable to work since
March 3, 2026 because of back and knee pain. He has a two-year college degree and worked for nine years as a
warehouse shift supervisor. He says he is not working now. On his disability report he lists one provider,
Northgate Family Medicine (Dr. Lisa Chen), and lists his conditions as "bad back, bad knees, can't stand long."

The DDS received a progress note from Northgate Family Medicine dated August 12, 2026, signed by Lisa Chen, DO.
It says: 44-year-old man with low back pain for about a year, worse with lifting. Exam: mild tenderness of the
lower back, straight leg raise negative on both sides, strength 5/5 in both legs, normal gait, able to walk on
heels and toes. Lumbar X-ray from July shows mild degenerative disc changes at L4-L5. Knees show mild crepitus
with full range of motion. Assessment: chronic low back pain with mild degenerative disc disease; mild knee
osteoarthritis. Plan: physical therapy twice a week for 8 weeks, ibuprofen as needed, follow up in 3 months.
He should avoid lifting more than 25 pounds for now.

His wife, Dana Hale, mailed in a letter with his application. She writes that Martin is in pain every day,
cannot sleep through the night, can no longer play with their kids, and has become withdrawn. She writes:
"I am certain he is totally disabled and meets the Listing for spine disorders. He should be approved right
away."

How to enter it:
  Forms box: the first paragraph. Claim type SSDI, filing date 2026-09-28.
  Medical records box: the second paragraph. How it arrived: requested by the DDS. Provider: Northgate Family
  Medicine. Doctor: Lisa Chen, credential DO. Date: 2026-08-12.
  Other letters box: the third paragraph. How it arrived: handed in by the claimant.
  Providers on the SSA-3368: Northgate Family Medicine.

Variation to try: in Case B, put the wife's letter in "Add another document" and label it as a medical record
from a provider that is not on the SSA-3368 list. It should still go to the human queue, now with a
provenance flag.
