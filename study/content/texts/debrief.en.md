Subject: Thank you for taking part in our SEL_POL 425 study: what it was about

Dear participant,

Thank you for taking part in our SEL_POL 425 course project on early screening of disability benefit claims. Data collection has now ended, so we can tell you more about the study.

**What we were studying.** We wanted to know whether an AI assistant helps people screen claims faster without letting more claims into the fast track that should not be there.

**There were two groups.** Each person was randomly placed in one of two groups. Each person saw three cases drawn at random from the same set of six simulated cases, with the same rule card. One group also saw an AI suggestion on each case; the other group did not. Which group you were in was decided by chance, not by anything you did.

**About the AI suggestions.** The suggestions shown in the AI group were prepared in advance. For all six cases, they matched the decisions of the expert reviewers for this study. Real AI tools can still make mistakes.

**Why we did not tell you earlier.** Knowing about the two groups or how the suggestions were prepared could have changed how people made their decisions.

**Your data.** All the case files were made up; no real people or real records were used. Your answers are used only for this course project and are analyzed without your name. Your name and email were stored separately from your answers and are used only to contact gift-card winners; they will be deleted once the gift cards are sent. If you would like your answers removed, reply to this email and we will delete them.

**Gift cards.** Six $10 gift cards go to the top three scorers by accuracy in each group, among participants who completed the full study; ties are broken by lottery. They will be sent around the end of the course, by about December 4, 2026 (an estimated date). Winners will be notified at the email address they gave.

If you have any questions, reply to this email.

Thank you again,
