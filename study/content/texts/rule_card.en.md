# SSDI Fast-Track Rule Card

In this simulation, you are an initial reviewer at a state disability determination agency for Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI). Use the application and medical records to decide whether to recommend the claim for fast-track review.

- **Fast track (Yes):** The file is complete enough to clearly establish eligibility under this study’s rules. The claim gets priority so it can be reviewed sooner.
- **Regular review (No):** The file does not clearly establish eligibility or the claim does not meet the fast-track criteria. The claim goes through regular review, and more information may be requested. This does not deny benefits.

### 1. Check the condition: What do the medical records confirm?

| Condition | What to look for in this study |
| --- | --- |
| Spinal cord injury | The records must clearly show **complete loss of function in both legs** due to a spinal cord injury. A back injury, leg weakness, or wheelchair use alone is not enough. |
| Lung cancer | The records must confirm **small cell lung cancer**. Suspected lung cancer, a mass in the lung, or lung cancer with no type specified needs further review. |
| Leg sprain | A leg sprain alone does not meet this study’s fast-track criteria. |
| Low back pain | Low back pain alone does not meet this study’s fast-track criteria. |

### 2. Check the source: Who confirms the key medical finding?

| Evidence source | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Medical records the state agency obtained directly from a hospital or clinic | **Use them to confirm the condition.** The agency can verify which provider supplied the records. |
| Materials submitted by the applicant or statements from family members | **Use for reference only.** If the key medical finding appears only in these materials, the claim goes to regular review. |

### 3. Check the duration: How long has the condition affected the ability to work?

| Condition | Duration requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Spinal cord injury | Both legs have completely lost function for **at least 3 continuous months**, counted from when the loss of function began. A shorter or unclear duration needs further review. |
| Small cell lung cancer | Once records obtained directly from the provider confirm the diagnosis, there is **no need to wait 3 months**. |

Recommend fast-track review only when the condition, source, and applicable duration requirements are all met.
