### Gift cards

Six **$10 gift cards** will be given out: **three to the top scorers in each of the two groups**, based on accuracy (how many of your assigned cases you answer correctly). To be eligible, you must complete the full study. Answers given implausibly fast may be treated as invalid. Ties are broken by lottery. Each email address may take part once. Email addresses will be deleted once the gift cards are sent.
