## COMMENT
The cores show sheets and nests of small to intermediate-sized tumor cells with scant cytoplasm, finely granular "salt and pepper" chromatin, inconspicuous nucleoli and nuclear molding. There is extensive crush artifact, abundant apoptotic debris and geographic necrosis. Mitoses exceed 10 per 2 mm2.

The tumor cells are positive for AE1/AE3 (dot-like), synaptophysin, INSM1, chromogranin (focal) and TTF-1 (diffuse, strong); negative for CK20, CDX2, p40, napsin A and hepatocyte antigen (HepPar-1). Ki-67 proliferation index is approximately 90%. These findings are diagnostic of small cell carcinoma (high-grade neuroendocrine carcinoma, small cell type). In conjunction with the CT findings of a right hilar mass with bulky mediastinal adenopathy, this is consistent with metastasis from a primary small cell lung carcinoma.
